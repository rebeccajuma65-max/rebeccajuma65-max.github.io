Field Chronicles

Stories From Across Sudan

Filter through inspiring testimonies of leadership, entrepreneurship, and sisterhood.

Education
Kassala • By Alaa, Age 21

The Classroom Beneath the Acacia Tree

When formal high schools shut their doors, university student Alaa established an outdoor learning circle. Equipped with solar lanterns and tablets, she prepares 45 teenage girls for their secondary exams.

✨ 45 Girls Kept Learning
Enterprise
River Nile • By Zainab & Collective

Spinning Cotton Into Sovereign Livelihoods

Combining ancient Sudanese loom artistry with modern design, Zainab’s collective turned a small seed loan into a thriving textile guild that sustains 12 families without relying on food handouts.

✨ 12 Households Self-Sustaining
Leadership
Al Jazirah • By Fatima & Rania

Two Mothers Who Broke the Cycle of Feud

When local tensions threatened to turn neighboring farming communities against each other, Fatima and Rania led a joint water-sharing council that restored peace and established shared grain reserves.

✨ 1,200 Residents Protected
Health
Darfur Enclave • By Maryam, Midwife

Midnight Deliveries in the Shadow of Crisis

Traveling by foot through unpaved valleys with our mobile maternal emergency kit, midwife Maryam has brought 140 healthy babies into the world without losing a single mother to preventable hemorrhage.

✨ 140 Zero-Complication Births
Youth
Port Sudan • By Safia, Age 19

The Solar-Powered Digital Guild

In a crowded transit center, Safia built a coding and translation workshop powered by two solar panels. Today, 60 young women earn international freelance wages to support their displaced parents.

✨ 60 Girls Earning Remotely
Leadership
Khartoum • By Counselor Rawia

Advocating for the Unheard at Dawn

Defending women unjustly targeted under emergency decrees, Rawia’s volunteer legal team successfully secured releases for 85 arbitrary detainees and established legal safehouses for GBV survivors.

✨ 85 Freedom Rulings Won

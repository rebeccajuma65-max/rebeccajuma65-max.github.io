Our Programs & Grassroots Services

Delivering rapid humanitarian protection alongside long-term systemic support to empower Sudanese women to rebuild communities, achieve financial self-reliance, and lead civic change.

Emergency Response

Safe Havens & Dignity Aid Kits

Providing urgent emergency food baskets, clean water purification tabs, and comprehensive maternal hygiene and dignity kits to internally displaced women fleeing active conflict zones.

📦 18,200 Kits Delivered
Education & Youth

Kandaka Girls Scholarship Fund

Ensuring girls displaced from school receive full tuition, books, solar study lanterns, and digital learning modules to keep their academic dreams alive during crises.

🎓 1,420 Girls Back in Class
Economic Autonomy

Artisan & Agro-Business Co-Ops

Supplying seed micro-grants ($150–$500), business literacy mentoring, and direct market linkage for women producing textiles, culinary goods, and sustainable soap.

💼 420+ Co-Ops Operational
Healthcare & Wellness

Mobile Maternal Clinics & Healing

Mobile clinics staffed by courageous Sudanese midwives providing prenatal checkups, clean birthing kits, and trauma-informed mental healing circles for survivors.

🩺 8,900+ Medical Visits
Human Rights

Legal Defense & GBV Advocacy

Pro-bono legal counsel, emergency protection relocation, and international treaty documentation for women facing violence, land dispossession, or systemic discrimination.

⚖️ 530+ Survivors Protected
Civic Leadership

Inter-Tribal Peace Circles

Convening women elders, youth activists, and regional leaders from all states of Sudan to negotiate local ceasefires, de-escalate community friction, and foster reconciliation.

🕊️ 18 Active Peace Circles
